# Roadmap — planned, not current functionality

1.0.0 preserves the technically accepted RC2 features. No new runtime features.
MIT/free/open source. No artificial limits, license activation, shop or subscription.

After 1.0: PB, theoretical PB from sector bests, optional local XML results,
fuel log and setup notes in the popup. No dates or accuracy guarantees. Unknown
or invalid laps must never be silently treated as valid official bests.

Possible later Pro work is an idea, not an implemented product or offer. Any reuse
must respect MIT/contributor/upstream licenses; no exclusive contributor ownership
or removal of MIT permissions is implied. No payment promise or invented price.
